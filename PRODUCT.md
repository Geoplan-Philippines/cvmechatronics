# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Residential homeowners and commercial property owners in the Philippines. Both arrive at the same decision point: they're evaluating a significant installation — gate automation, smart glass, security cameras, centralized control — and they need to trust the company before committing. Residential clients are typically middle-to-upper income homeowners wanting to upgrade their property. Commercial clients are business owners, office managers, and property developers. Both segments are skeptical, time-limited, and convert on confidence.

## Product Purpose

CV Mechatronics installs mechatronic systems that automate and secure residential and commercial properties: sliding/swing/roll-up gate motors, PDLC smart glass tint, CCTV and automated lighting, and app-based centralized control. The landing page exists to convert visitor into inquiry — a free quote request or direct contact. Success is a visitor who feels confident enough to reach out.

## Brand Personality

Precise. Restrained. Trustworthy. A company that earns confidence not through flashiness but through competence. The voice is calm and expert — the way a skilled technician explains something complex without condescension. Aspirational without pretension: modern living, engineered properly. Local company, world-class standard.

## Anti-references

- The AI slop reference landing page (cvmechatronics-landing-page/): dark glassmorphism, indigo/cyan glow effects, glass-blur cards. Reads as generic SaaS, not a skilled local installer.
- Cyberpunk/neon aesthetics — signal "tech startup," not "reliable professional service."
- Warm beige/cream AI-default palettes — safe, bland, invisible in a crowded category.
- SaaS dashboard clichés: hero metric grids, identical icon+card grids, numbered section scaffolding (01/02/03 eyebrows).

## Design Principles

1. **Restraint is the signal** — premium means editing, not adding. Every element earns its place or it's gone.
2. **Show the outcome, not the machine** — a family coming home to an automated gate beats a technical diagram. The service is about life quality, not gadgetry.
3. **Competence through precision** — typography, spacing, and hierarchy communicate expertise. Nothing looks accidental.
4. **Local but world-class** — feels like a real Filipino company that delivers at international standards, not a foreign brand template or a generic bootstrap site.
5. **Trust through clarity** — information architecture and copy make the next step obvious. Confusion costs inquiries.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text ≥4.5:1 contrast. Large text ≥3:1. Reduced motion respected via `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`. No content gated behind animation completion.
